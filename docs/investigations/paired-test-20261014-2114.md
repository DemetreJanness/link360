hips are confidently visible but their midpoint lies above the halfway height
of that box, the pose cannot qualify as the torso. This is a conservative
engineering plausibility check, not a universal anatomical model. It may reject
some valid unusual poses; Acer can guide while the Link is uncertain.

## Framing-director requirement from user steering

Accepted direction: short bounded prediction; promptly widen for fast motion,
edge risk or unreliable close-view torso; then restore torso composition slowly
after stable movement. A local director policy should coordinate motion and
framing while manual actions override it. Prediction needs confidence, bounded
horizon and mapped-hull checks; camera motion must not be mistaken for subject
motion. Native/software camera controllers remain mutually exclusive.

This director/automatic zoom behaviour is NOT implemented in the current build.
Continuous tracking currently fixes zoom and rejects zoom changes. Implementing
it requires a coordinated zoom servo and test coverage, not repeatedly stopping
tracking to call the manual zoom action. First validate corrected target lock
and Acer/Link agreement so a new policy does not chase false pose coordinates.
